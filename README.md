Preparing Your Home for a Precise Move
A careful crew works best when you share your preferences early. Describe the layout of your new home and point out items that need extra protection, such as glass tables, antiques or electronics. Good furniture protection begins with proper wrapping, but it also depends on clear pathways and reserved lifts, so check access and parking with building management. Keep valuables, documents and a first night bag with you. Book one to two weeks ahead, and earlier for weekends, public holidays and month end. 

Most residential moves take about three to four hours once loading begins. Your preparation list:
• Share photos of delicate or valuable items during the assessment
 • Describe your preferred room by room layout
 • Reserve lifts and parking before moving day
 • Label every carton clearly by room
 • Keep valuables and documents with you
